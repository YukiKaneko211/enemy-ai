# Enemy Ai

FPS shooter video game features the enemy AI that makes own decisions depending on the situation, made by Unreal Engine 5.

This app was created according to the requirements of kood/Jõhvi: https://github.com/01-edu/public/tree/master/subjects/gaming/zombie-ai

## How to Play

To play the game, execute `ZombieAi.exe` in the project folder.

- move around by WASD
- look around by mouse
- shoot by left click 

You can also check the test play video.
